Shelly Control Systems - Our Specialization

Why Shelly?

Shelly is one of the most popular and reliable smart home brands in Europe:

  • Compact – fits into standard recessed mounting boxes
  • Universal – works with any electrical installation (no proprietary standards)
  • Wifi – no hubs or additional equipment needed
  • Open source – supports Home Assistant, Alexa, Google Home, MQTT
  • Local control – works even without internet connection
  • OTA updates – remote firmware updates
  • Energy monitoring – many models monitor electricity consumption

Popular Shelly Devices

Shelly 1 / 1PM / Plus 1PM

  • 1 channel relay to turn on/off any device
  • Ideal for lighting, outlets, heaters
  • PM version measures electricity consumption
  • Plus 1PM – newest version with Bluetooth

Shelly Dimmer 2

  • Light intensity control (0-100%)
  • Suitable for LED, halogen, incandescent lamps
  • Calibratable for specific lighting type
  • Energy consumption monitoring

Shelly 2.5

  • 2 channel relay or curtain/roller shutter control
  • Modes: 2 separate switches or UP/DOWN control
  • Overload protection
  • Energy measurement for both channels
