What is Electrical Panel Assembly?

Electrical panel assembly is one of the most crucial stages of electrical installation, ensuring safe and reliable distribution of electrical energy in your home or business premises. The electrical panel is the main control center of your electrical system, housing circuit breakers, protective relays, and other critical components that protect your electrical network from overloads and short circuits.

Our team offers comprehensive electrical panel assembly services – from initial consultation and design to final installation and documentation submission. We work with both residential homes and business facilities, providing customized solutions for each client.

How Does the Electrical Panel Assembly Process Work?

1. Initial Meeting and Needs Analysis

Everything starts with a detailed conversation with the client. Our specialist visits the site, performs a thorough inspection, and clarifies all your needs. We discuss:

  • Number and power of planned electrical consumers
  • Expected electrical load and energy consumption
  • Special requirements (generator, solar panels, smart systems)
  • Safety requirements and regulatory compliance
  • Budget constraints and preferences

2. Electrical Panel Design

Based on collected information, we prepare a detailed electrical panel design. We determine:

  • Appropriate panel size and type (temporary, main electrical panel, automation panel, transformer panel)
  • Number and characteristics of required circuit breakers
  • Configuration of protective relays (RCD, RCBO)
  • Internal component layout diagram
  • Additional equipment (meters, contactors, impulse relays)

3. Quality Component Selection

We work only with equipment from trusted manufacturers – ABB, noark, eti, Hager. We select components based on:

  • Lithuanian electrical energy standards (LST EN 61439)
  • Optimal price-quality ratio
  • Long-term reliability and warranties
  • Easy maintenance and component availability

4. Professional Assembly

Panel assembly is performed in our workshops or on-site, depending on project complexity. The assembly process includes:

  • Precise component arrangement according to diagram
  • Professional wire installation with labeling
  • Verification and protection of all connections
  • Panel testing before installation
  • Detailed installation documentation

5. On-Site Installation

We deliver the assembled electrical panel to the site and perform:

  • Safe panel mounting on wall
  • Connection and labeling of all lines
  • Grounding system connection
  • Final operational check with measurements
  • System testing and adjustment

Electrical Panel Maintenance

Regular electrical panel maintenance ensures long-term safe operation and protects against unexpected failures. We recommend annual maintenance. Our maintenance services include:

  • Visual inspection – check component condition, non-functioning elements, signs of corrosion
  • Resistance measurements – ensure all connections are securely tightened
  • Operation tests – check circuit breaker and protective relay functionality
  • Thermal imaging inspection – identify potential overload locations
  • Cleaning work – remove dust and dirt
  • Documentation updates – record maintenance results

Panel Configuration and Modernization

As electricity consumption needs change, it's often necessary to adapt existing electrical panels. We perform:

  • Installation of additional circuit breakers for new lines
  • Generator and solar power plant integration
  • Smart control system connection (Shelly, KNX)
  • Replacement of old components with modern ones
  • Panel expansion or replacement with larger one
