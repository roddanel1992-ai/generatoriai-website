Generator Integration with Hybrid Solar Inverter

Full energy independence – solar, battery and generator in one solution

Do you have a solar power plant with a hybrid inverter and want to ensure electricity supply even when there is neither sunshine nor grid? We integrate generators with the most popular hybrid solar inverters – a solution that allows your home or business to operate completely autonomously.

Why integrate a generator with a hybrid inverter?

  • Generator ensures electricity supply at night or during longer cloudy periods when the battery is discharged
  • The system operates automatically – no manual switching
  • Generator fuel is used only when really needed – you save on fuel
  • Full energy independence – combining solar energy, battery and generator

Which inverters do we work with?

We integrate generators with the following hybrid inverters most popular in Lithuania:

  • Huawei SUN2000 – one of the most popular in Lithuania, has a dedicated generator input
  • Fronius GEN24 Plus – high-quality Austrian manufacturer, wide integration possibilities
  • Solax X1 Hybrid / X3 Hybrid – flexible and economical solution
  • Sungrow SH series – popular worldwide, reliable operation
  • Sofar Solar hybrid models – full island mode (off-grid) support

Using a different inverter? Contact us – we'll discuss the possibilities.

How does integration work?

  • We assess your existing inverter model and its capabilities
  • We select a suitable generator according to the inverter's technical requirements (synchronization, frequency, voltage)
  • We install the generator and connect it to the inverter's generator input
  • We configure automatic switching – the system decides when to start the generator
  • We perform full system testing and train you to use it
