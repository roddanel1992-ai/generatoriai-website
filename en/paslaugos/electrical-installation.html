Electrical Installation – Professional Electrical Work

From new wiring to modernization of old electrical systems

We perform all types of electrical installation work – from new wiring to modernization of old electrical systems. Certified electrical specialists, work according to applicable Lithuanian standards, complete documentation.

Services Provided

Installation of new electrical systems

  • Electrical wiring in homes, apartments, commercial premises
  • Electrical panel assembly and installation
  • Socket, switch and lighting fixture connection
  • Three-phase line installation
  • Underground cable laying outdoors

Modernization of existing installations

  • Replacement of old aluminum wires with copper
  • Electrical panel renewal – new automatic switches, protections
  • Adding additional sockets and lines
  • EV Charger connection for electric vehicles

Specialized electrical work

  • Electrical installation connection with generator and ATS (ATD)
  • Creating separate electrical circuits (for refrigerator, heating, server room)
  • Lightning protection system installation
  • Smart home electrical foundation preparation

Our Work Principles

  • All work performed according to LST HD 60364 electrical installation standard
  • We use only certified cables and equipment
  • An electrical work certificate is issued after the work (if required)
  • Tidy finishing – cables hidden, connections insulated, premises clean
  • 1-year warranty on all work performed
