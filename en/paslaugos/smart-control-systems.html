Modern Generator Management

Today's technology allows you to control your generator not just manually, but also completely automatically or even remotely via a smartphone. A smart control system ensures that the generator starts exactly when needed, notifies you of problems and allows you to monitor everything in real time – whether you're at home, work or traveling abroad.

Our team installs smart control systems adapted to both new and existing generators. From simple automatic startup to fully integrated Smart Home system – we offer solutions for every need and budget.

Smart Control Features

  • Automatic startup – generator starts automatically when power goes out
  • Remote control – control generator from your smartphone
  • Push notifications – alerts about startup, problems, low fuel
  • Real-time monitoring – view generator status anytime
  • Operating statistics – track hours and fuel consumption
  • Scheduled maintenance – automatic service reminders
  • Load management – automatic load distribution

System Benefits

  • Peace of mind – system works 24/7 without your intervention
  • Cost savings – optimized fuel consumption and maintenance schedules
  • Reliability – immediate alert if problems occur
  • Convenience – no need to be at home to monitor generator
  • Integration – connects with your existing smart home system

Installation Options

  • Basic – automatic startup only
  • Standard – automatic startup + push notifications
  • Premium – full remote control + full monitoring
  • Enterprise – multiple generators management + advanced analytics
