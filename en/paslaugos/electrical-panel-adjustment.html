Electrical Panel Adjustment for Generator

Safe automatic switching from grid to generator

Why Electrical Panel Adjustment is Required?

To safely use a generator as a backup power source, it is essential to properly adjust the existing electrical panel. Simply plugging a generator into an outlet is dangerous and illegal – you need a professional solution that ensures safe switching between grid power and the generator. This means installing an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS), implementing additional protection and possible panel modernization.

We work with all types of panels – from small household to large three-phase industrial panels. All work is performed according to Lithuanian electrical installation rules (EIT) and with 1-year warranty.

What Does Electrical Panel Adjustment Include?

  • ATS installation – automatic transfer switch installation in the main panel
  • Circuit breaker upgrade – adding new breakers for generator connection
  • Grounding system – proper grounding of the generator circuit
  • Protection installation – surge protectors and safety switches
  • Panel modernization – upgrade of old panel if needed
  • Cable routing – installation of cable conduits and ducts

Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS)

The heart of any generator system is the ATS. This device automatically:

  • Monitors grid voltage continuously
  • Detects power outage within seconds
  • Sends signal to generator to start
  • Automatically switches load to generator
  • Returns to grid when power restored
  • Allows generator to cool down before shutdown

Installation Process

Typically takes 1-2 days and includes:

  1. Detailed panel inspection and measurement
  2. ATS selection and circuit planning
  3. New breakers installation
  4. ATS connection and testing
  5. System verification in all modes
  6. Documentation and training
