Professional Sound Solutions for All Spaces

Quality sound creates atmosphere, improves mood, and makes any space more comfortable. Whether you want to install a home theater system in your living room, mount ceiling speakers throughout your house, or create a professional sound system for a restaurant – we offer optimal solutions for every situation.

We work with both private clients (apartments, houses, cottages) and business facilities (cafes, restaurants, shops, hotels, offices). Home Sound Solutions

Home Theater Systems

Create cinema atmosphere in your living room:

  • 5.1 / 7.1 / Dolby Atmos – surround sound systems with subwoofer
  • AV receiver – central audio control device with HDMI
  • Front speakers – Floor-standing or Bookshelf speakers
  • Center channel – for clear dialogues
  • Surround speakers – for immersive sound effect
  • Subwoofer – deep bass for cinematic effects
  • Cable routing – concealed wiring behind walls and ceilings

Multiroom Audio Systems

Enjoy music in all rooms:

  • Sonos ecosystem – wireless multiroom system (Play:1, Play:5, Arc, Beam)
  • Ceiling speakers – discrete and powerful (6.5" or 8" drivers)
  • Centralized control – management from single app
  • Zone management – different music in different rooms
  • Streaming integration – Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, Deezer
  • Voice control – Google Assistant, Alexa integration

Hi-Fi Stereo Systems

Audiophile music experience:

  • High-quality stereo amplifiers (Class A/AB/D)
  • Floor-standing speakers with excellent acoustics
  • Vinyl turntables (Technics, Pro-Ject, Audio-Technica)
  • DAC (Digital-to-Analog Converter) Hi-Res audio
  • Streaming DAC (Sonos Port, Bluesound Node)
  • Cables and accessories

Business Sound Solutions

PA (Public Address) Systems

Professional sound systems for public spaces:

  • 100V line systems – efficient power for long cables
  • Ceiling PA speakers – even sound distribution
  • Zone amplifiers – separate control for each zone
  • Mixers and processors – audio source management
  • Background music supply – via Spotify, USB, FM tuner
  • Announcement system – microphone for announcements

Restaurant and Cafe Sound Systems

Create cozy atmosphere in your establishment:

  • Discrete ceiling speakers (white/black color)
  • Zone management (different sound in hall, bar, terrace)
  • Bluetooth/Wifi control – easy music changes
  • Background music players
  • Automatic volume adjustment by time
