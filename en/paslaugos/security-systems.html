Alarm System Installation

Intrusion Detection Systems

Protect your property from unauthorized entry with the most modern alarm systems:

  • Motion detectors (PIR sensors) – detect human movement in rooms
  • Door and window magnetic sensors – register door/window opening
  • Glass break detectors – respond to glass breaking sound
  • Passive infrared beam detectors – perimeter protection for territory
  • Vibration sensors – detect building structure vibration
  • Central control panel – activates/deactivates system with password or card
  • Wireless remotes – quick system activation/deactivation
  • Sirens – audio and/or visual warning of intrusion

Wired and Wireless Systems

We offer both solutions depending on the property:

  • Wired alarm – more reliable, suitable for newly constructed buildings or major renovations
  • Wireless alarm – quick installation, ideal for existing living spaces, easy expansion
  • Hybrid system – combination of wired and wireless components for optimal results

GSM Modules and Monitoring Centers

Instant notification about incidents:

  • GSM module with SIM card – SMS/call notifications about danger
  • Push notifications to smartphone via mobile app
  • Integration with professional monitoring center (24/7 response)
  • Email notifications with event details
  • Remote system control via smartphone

Video Surveillance Systems

IP Camera Installation

Modern IP cameras provide high-quality video and various advanced features:

  • Full HD / 4K resolution – clear and detailed video day and night
  • Night vision (IR LED) – surveillance in darkness up to 30-50 meters
  • Wide Dynamic Range (WDR) – clear image in bright lighting conditions
  • PTZ cameras – remotely controlled camera rotation and zoom
  • Motion detection – recording only when motion detected (saves space)
  • Remote viewing – live video viewing from smartphone
  • Cloud storage – automatic backup to cloud

Indoor and Outdoor Cameras

  • Outdoor cameras – weather-resistant (IP65/IP66/IP67), durable construction
  • Indoor cameras – discrete design, excellent image quality
  • Dome cameras – vandal-resistant, suitable for public spaces
  • Bullet cameras – visible deterrent effect

Video Recording and Storage

Various recording solutions:

  • NVR (Network Video Recorder) – centralized recording for all cameras
  • Hard drives – local storage (1TB - 8TB)
  • Cloud storage – backup to cloud servers
  • Continuous or motion-based recording – optimize storage space
  • Archive retention – configurable recording retention period
