Generator for Apartment – Solution Without Additional Wiring

Inverter generator connected to the balcony socket – no additional wiring needed

Do you live in an apartment and think a generator is not accessible to you? Wrong! If there is an electrical socket on your apartment's balcony or terrace – you already have everything you need to use a generator during electrical outages.

How does it work?

A compact inverter generator is placed on the balcony. Using a special adapter (extension cable with reverse plug), it is connected to the balcony socket. This way, generator energy is supplied back to the apartment's electrical installation – lighting, refrigerator, chargers, and even some electric heaters work. When the generator is disconnected, the sockets return to standard sockets.

Advantages

  • No need to run any additional wiring – existing balcony socket is used
  • Compact generator (e.g., 1–2 kW inverter) fits on a standard balcony
  • The quiet inverter generator does not make much noise for neighbors
  • The solution is suitable for rentals – the generator is not tied to the apartment
  • Quick start – electricity in the apartment in a few seconds

The generator can also serve as an additional power source while waiting for power restoration.

What is needed?

  • Balcony socket (standard 230V)
  • Inverter generator (recommended: 1–3.5 kW)
  • Special reverse connection adapter (we manufacture and test)
  • Simple consultation with us – we will assess your apartment's electricity and confirm safety

Safety

This solution is used only in emergencies and only with installations protected by fuses. Before use, it is necessary to disconnect the apartment's main switch so that generator electricity does not enter the general apartment building network. We teach how to do this correctly and safely.

Important: this service is suitable only for apartments with a separate electrical distribution panel. We will verify this together during consultation.
