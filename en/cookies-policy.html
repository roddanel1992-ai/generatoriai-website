Cookies Policy

What cookies are used on this website and how to manage consent

Last updated: 2026-05-27

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device (computer, phone) to remember your choices, maintain your session, or collect statistical data about how the site is used.

This policy describes which cookies www.tavogeneratorius.lt uses, their purpose, lifespan, and how you can manage your consent.

2. Cookie categories

2.1. Strictly necessary cookies (always on)

Required for core website functionality and security. Without them the site will not function properly.

Cookie Provider Purpose Lifespan
cc_cookie tavogeneratorius.lt Stores your cookie consent preferences 6 months
__cf_bm Cloudflare Bot and DDoS attack protection 30 min

2.2. Analytics cookies (require consent)

Help us understand how visitors use the website. Google Analytics is currently not active, but if enabled it will use the cookies listed below.

Cookie Provider Purpose Lifespan
_ga Google Analytics Unique visitor identifier 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics Session state (GA4) 2 years
_gid Google Analytics Distinguishes visitors within 24 hours 24 hours

2.3. Functional cookies (require consent)

Enable additional features such as YouTube video playback.

Cookie Provider Purpose Lifespan
YSC, VISITOR_INFO1_LIVE YouTube (Google) Embedded video playback and statistics Session / 6 months

Note: we use the youtube-nocookie.com domain, which sets cookies only when you click "Play". Without consent no YouTube cookies are set.

3. How to manage consent

On your first visit you see the cookie consent dialog. You can:

  • Accept all – enable all categories
  • Reject all – only necessary cookies remain
  • Open settings – enable only categories you agree to

You can change your choice at any time by clicking the link below:

🍪 Open cookie settings

4. Managing cookies in the browser

You can also manage cookies directly via your browser's settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Important: if necessary cookies are disabled, some site features may not work correctly.
